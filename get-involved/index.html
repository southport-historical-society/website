Get Involved

There are so many ways to GET INVOLVED with the Southport Historical Society.

Join The Southport Historical Society

Perhaps the easiest way to donate to the Southport Historical Society is to become a member. We offer affordable membership levels for any budget.

Learn More

Purchase SHS Books

Choose from over twenty books about the historical aspects of Southport, North Carolina. Many of these books were written by members of the Southport Historical Society.

Learn More

Donate Your Time

We are always in need of volunteers! There are several areas where you can get involved and lend a hand. Why not investigate where your skills and interests can be matched to a meaningful volunteer position?

Learn More

Be a Speaker

The Southport Historical Society is looking for individuals who would like to share their expertise with our members and friends. Please consider speaking at one of our 2nd Tuesday Talks or participating in our Living Voices of the Past program.

Learn More

Write for the SHS

Do you have an idea for an interesting article about Southport history?

Explore the opportunities to contribute a written piece to the Whittler’s Bench Blog and Newsletter.

Learn More

Make a Financial Donation

Your donation helps to fund important historical restoration and community historical educational programs.

Find out how you can directly impact the preservation of Southport’s history!

Learn More
