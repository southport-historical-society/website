Donate to the Southport Historical Society

We’re grateful that you’re considering a donation to support the important preservation and education work of the Southport Historical Society.

Donations may be made by Check or Online

Follow this link to donate online. If you prefer to donate by check, please send it to:

Southport Historical Society

Donate historic Southport artifacts

If you have historic artifacts that may be a potential resource that you would like to gift to the Southport Historical Society for preservation and the educational benefit of the community, please contact us at info@southporthistoricalsociety.org for more information on the types of artifact donations that may be accepted.

Donate through your Estate Plan

A gift through your will or living trust provides the Society with the fiscal foundation that ensures the continued strength of our programs and services. In addition, there are other charitable gift planning opportunities that may help you meet your financial goals while also securing our future.

  • A Southport Historical Society Charitable Gift Annuity offers generous, dependable payments for life. You can support our organization while enjoying tax benefits and possibly, increased income, for yourself or another.
  • Naming the Southport Historical Society as a beneficiary of your retirement accounts, security accounts or life insurance policies is a wonderful way to provide a future benefit to the SHS which costs you nothing during your lifetime.
