The Commemorative Brick program is the official way for those who feel a connection to Southport to memorialize a person, organization, event or place of personal significance. Take a stroll along Ft. Johnston’s walkway to see bricks that commemorate birthdays, anniversaries, retirement, home ownership, graduations, friendships, and of course, the loss of loved ones. Community organizations and businesses are recognized.

The Commemorative Brick Program is an opportunity to add your personalized support to help the society continue to play an important role in keeping Southport’s history alive.

Please support the Southport Historical Society, as well as the City of Southport, in preserving our rich history while commemorating a special person, organization, or event of personal significance to you!

New commemorative brick orders are accepted October 1st through November 30th. New bricks are laid in the Spring.

Use our new Brick Finder tool to purchase bricks, search and browse bricks, and find their locations.
